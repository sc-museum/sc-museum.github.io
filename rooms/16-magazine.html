Issue 2 · Spring 2021

The Military Police School at Camp Gordon

After World War II, Camp Gordon became home to a disciplinary barracks and, in 1948, the Military Police School. This issue covers that era, prisoners of war in Georgia, and Spc. Maurice J. Johnson of the 501st Signal Battalion, killed in Mosul in 2003. Page 16 is blank in the original, and the donation page with bank details is left out.
