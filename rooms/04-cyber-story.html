The Cyber Story

The Army's youngest branch, built on the Signal Corps' oldest instinct.

A new branch, an old mission

Army Cyber Command stood up its operational headquarters activities and the Cyber Center of Excellence at Fort Gordon starting in 2013, consolidating the Army's cyber, electronic warfare, and information operations training alongside the Signal School. In September 2018, Cyber became the Army's newest basic branch — the first new branch created since Special Forces in 1987.

The crest above — a shield split in black and white beneath a raised dagger, bearing the words Defend, Attack, Exploit — belongs to the Cyber Center of Excellence, and captures the branch's mission in three words: defend Army networks, attack when ordered, and exploit the advantage that information gives a modern force.
