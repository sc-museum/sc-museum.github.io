The campaign: a building for the collection

The Signal Corps Museum on post closed on 25 February 2021, when base growth and construction took its building. The collection — the artifacts behind every room in this virtual museum — has been in storage ever since.

The Society is raising funds to buy a building just outside the gates of Fort Gordon and turn it back into a museum. About $500,000 has been raised so far by more than 80 donors. Once the building is secured, renovation follows, then the exhibits, then the doors open to the public again. Preserving this history matters for training today's Soldiers, educating the public, and preparing young Americans for service.
