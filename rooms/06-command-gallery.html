Signal Commands

The six Signal Commands are the Army's largest communications formations. They oversee brigades, battalions, and other units, and theater Signal Commands are typically led by one- or two-star generals. Select a patch to read the unit's official lineage.

Featured unit

501st Signal Battalion

"The Voice of the Eagle" — the signal battalion of the 101st Airborne Division (Air Assault). It carried the division's communications through the opening year of Operation Iraqi Freedom under Lt. Col. Welton Chase, Jr., and was inactivated in 2004 when the 101st converted to the Army's modular brigade structure. On 5 June 2026 the Army reactivated the 501st as the division's signal battalion at Fort Campbell, Kentucky, in a change-of-command ceremony attended by veterans of every era back to Vietnam.
