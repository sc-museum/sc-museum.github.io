The Hello Girls

223 women. One war. A story nearly lost.

In 1918, the U.S. Army Signal Corps sent 223 women to France as telephone operators to help win the First World War. Fluent in French and English and trained on the Army's own switchboards, they connected the calls that moved orders along the front — some working within range of artillery fire. Told through letters, photographs, rare archival footage, and interviews with family and historians, their story remained almost entirely unknown for a century.

One of the Signal Corps Female Telephone Operators Unit, in uniform with service stripes.
Legislation

S.815 — Hello Girls Congressional Gold Medal Act of 2023 ↗

A bill to award the Congressional Gold Medal, collectively, to the female telephone operators of the Army Signal Corps known as the "Hello Girls," in recognition of their service during the First World War.
