Map is stylized and not to scale; times are approximate. The message is spelled in the July 1864 General Service Code used in the museum's Flag Drill (1 = flag to the signalman's right, 2 = to his left, 3 = front, ending a word). At Gettysburg in 1863 an earlier code was still in use, so this shows the method, not the exact signals sent that day. The music is an original score your browser plays as you watch; the narration uses your device's built-in voice.
