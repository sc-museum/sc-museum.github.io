The message is spelled in the July 1864 General Service Code used in the museum's Flag Drill (1 = flag to the signalman's right, 2 = to his left, 3 = front, ending a word). At Gettysburg in 1863 an earlier code was still in use, so this shows the method, not the exact signals sent that day.
