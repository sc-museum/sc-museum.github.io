Activation and Change of Command, 5 June 2026

101st Airborne Division (Air Assault) · McAuliffe Hall, Fort Campbell, Kentucky

In a single ceremony the 101st Airborne Division inactivated the 21st Brigade Engineer Battalion and activated the 501st Division Signal Battalion in its place, with command passing to the first commander of the reactivated 501st. The program is re-set below page for page, as printed.
