1st Signal Brigade

Organized in Vietnam on April 1, 1966, the 1st Signal Brigade grew into the largest Signal command of that war and has served in the Republic of Korea since 1972. These are the commanders and command sergeants major who have led it.

Listed as published by the 1st Signal Brigade Association, which keeps photographs of each leader; corrections go to the association's webmaster.

Where the Army publishes an official biography for a commander, that commander's name links to it. The Army's public register is the General Officer Management Office ↗, which lists only officers still serving — so of the general officers named above, only BG Wiersgalla has one. The brigade's Vietnam-era commanders (Terry, Van Harlingen, Rienzi, Foster, Weaver, Myer) commanded decades before the Army put biographies online, and no official versions exist on the web; the museum would welcome copies for the archive.
